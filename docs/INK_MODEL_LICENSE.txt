Handwritten digit model and test fixtures

MNIST authors: Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and Christopher J. C. Burges.
Dataset reference: https://keras.io/api/datasets/mnist/
Original dataset: http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/
Official Keras mirror: https://storage.googleapis.com/tensorflow/tf-keras-datasets/mnist.npz

MNIST is supplied under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The bundled ink-model.bin is a newly trained 784-input, 64-ReLU, 10-softmax
digit classifier, distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 terms. The small
MNIST test excerpt in tests/fixtures/mnist-digits.bin uses the same terms.
This notice applies to these model/data assets, not the app's other source
files or the separately attributed clinical reference PDFs.

Model changes: trained for 24 epochs with scikit-learn MLPClassifier, seed 27.
10,000 isolated held-out MNIST digits: 97.27% classification accuracy.
This benchmark does not establish accuracy for natural multi-digit writing,
decimal recognition, segmentation, medical use, or any tablet/stylus.

All application recognition runs locally. No patient ink is sent to an OCR API.
The displayed numeric reading is a draft until the clinician reviews and saves.
Training reproduction: see tools/train-ink-model.py and README.md.
